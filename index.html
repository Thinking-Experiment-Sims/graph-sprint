Slope Rules (x → v → a)

Remember: Slope takes you down from Position to Velocity to Acceleration.

  • x–t → v–t (Slope of Position is Velocity):
    • Tangent slope is positive ⇒ v > 0 (above zero time axis).
    • Tangent slope is negative ⇒ v < 0 (below zero time axis).
    • Horizontal tangent / peak / valley ⇒ v = 0 (instantaneous rest / turnaround).
  • v–t → a–t (Slope of Velocity is Acceleration):
    • Rising straight line (/) ⇒ constant positive acceleration (+a).
    • Falling straight line (\) ⇒ constant negative acceleration (−a).
    • Horizontal line (—) ⇒ zero acceleration (a = 0).
💡

Area & Concavity Shortcuts

Remember: Area takes you up from Acceleration to Velocity to Position.

  • Area Under Curves:
    • Area under a–t curve between t1 and t2 = Δv (change in velocity).
    • Area under v–t curve between t1 and t2 = Δx (displacement).
  • Concavity on x–t:
    • Concave up (∪, smiling curve): Positive acceleration (a > 0).
    • Concave down (∩, frowning curve): Negative acceleration (a < 0).
    • Straight line (no curve): Zero acceleration (a = 0).
  • Speeding Up vs Slowing Down:
    • Same signs (v > 0, a > 0 OR v < 0, a < 0) ⇒ speeding up (moving away from v = 0).
    • Opposite signs (v > 0, a < 0 OR v < 0, a > 0) ⇒ slowing down (approaching v = 0).
